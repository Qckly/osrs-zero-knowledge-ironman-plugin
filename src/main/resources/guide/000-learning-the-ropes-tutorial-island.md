# 000 — Learning the Ropes: Tutorial Island

**Status:** AUDITED V1  
**Route:** MANDATORY  
**Account:** Standard Ironman  
**Start:** Character Creator on Tutorial Island  
**Finish:** Arrive in Lumbridge as a Standard Ironman  
**Reward:** 1 Quest Point + mainland access + starter equipment

> Treat this entire guide as one giant quest. Complete one step, satisfy its **Complete when** condition, then continue.

## Step format

- **TARGET** — what the plugin should point to/highlight.
- **DO** — exact action for the player.
- **COMPLETE WHEN** — state that allows automatic/manual advance.
- **OPTIONAL** — never blocks the main route.

---

## A — Character creation

### 000.01 — Finish character creation
**TARGET:** Character Creator interface  
**DO:** Choose your appearance and name, then finish character creation. Appearance has no effect on stats or progression and can be changed later.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You are standing in the first Tutorial Island house.

### 000.02 — Choose the new-player path
**TARGET:** Familiarity/experience question  
**DO:** When asked how familiar you are with Old School RuneScape, choose the answer that says you are **brand new / new to the game**. Do not choose an experienced-player skip.  
**WHY:** This keeps the complete tutorial enabled.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You can continue with the Gielinor Guide.

### 000.03 — Talk to the Gielinor Guide
**TARGET:** Gielinor Guide  
**DO:** Left-click **Talk-to Gielinor Guide** and continue through the required dialogue/tutorial prompts.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** The control guide tells you to leave through the door.

### 000.04 — Leave the first house
**TARGET:** East door  
**DO:** Left-click the door, then follow the path to the Survival Expert.  
**BEGINNER NOTE:** Left-clicking the ground moves your character. Right-clicking something opens its available actions.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You are beside the Survival Expert and pond.

---

## B — Survival Expert

### 000.05 — Talk to the Survival Expert
**TARGET:** Survival Expert / Brynna  
**DO:** Talk to Brynna. She gives you a **small fishing net** and asks you to view it in your inventory.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Small fishing net is in your inventory.

### 000.06 — Open your inventory
**TARGET:** Backpack / Inventory icon  
**DO:** Click the backpack icon when it flashes.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Your inventory is visible and the tutorial directs you to fish.

### 000.07 — Catch one raw shrimp
**TARGET:** Fishing spot in the nearby pond  
**REQUIRED:** Small fishing net in inventory  
**DO:** Left-click the fishing spot and wait until you catch **1 raw shrimps**.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Raw shrimps is in your inventory.

### 000.08 — Open the Skills interface
**TARGET:** Skills icon  
**DO:** Click the flashing Skills icon after receiving Fishing XP.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** The Skills interface opens and the tutorial allows you to continue.

### 000.09 — Talk to Brynna again
**TARGET:** Survival Expert / Brynna  
**DO:** Talk to her again. Receive the **bronze axe** and **tinderbox**.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Bronze axe and tinderbox are both in your inventory.

### 000.10 — Chop one tree
**TARGET:** Any normal Tree in the survival area  
**REQUIRED:** Bronze axe  
**DO:** Left-click a Tree and wait until you receive **1 logs**.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Logs are in your inventory.

### 000.11 — Light the logs
**TARGET:** Logs in inventory  
**REQUIRED:** Tinderbox + logs  
**DO:** Use the tinderbox on the logs. If the game offers a direct **Light** action on the logs, that is also valid.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** A fire is burning on the ground.

### 000.12 — Cook the shrimp
**TARGET:** Your fire  
**REQUIRED:** Raw shrimps  
**DO:** Use the raw shrimps on the fire. If the shrimp burns, catch another raw shrimp, make another fire if necessary, and try again.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You have successfully cooked **shrimps** and the tutorial lets you leave this section.

### OPTIONAL 000-A — Extra Tutorial Island survival XP
**OPTIONAL:** You may train Fishing, Woodcutting, Firemaking and Cooking up to **level 3** here. Stop each selected skill immediately at level 3.  
**MAIN ROUTE:** Skip this. Mainland progression is more important than remaining here for tiny XP gains.

---

## C — Master Chef

### 000.13 — Enter the next building
**TARGET:** Door/building on the tutorial path  
**DO:** Follow the control-guide/path marker to the Master Chef and enter the building.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You can see the Master Chef.

### 000.14 — Talk to the Master Chef
**TARGET:** Master Chef  
**DO:** Talk to him until you receive the bread-making ingredients: **pot of flour** and **bucket of water**.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Both ingredients are available.

### 000.15 — Make bread dough
**TARGET:** Pot of flour / bucket of water  
**DO:** Use the pot of flour on the bucket of water, or the bucket of water on the pot of flour.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You have **bread dough**.

### 000.16 — Bake the bread
**TARGET:** Cooking range in the Master Chef's building  
**REQUIRED:** Bread dough  
**DO:** Use the bread dough on the range.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You have **bread** and the route to the Quest Guide is available.

---

## D — Quest Guide

### 000.17 — Enter the Quest Guide building
**TARGET:** Next building on the tutorial path  
**DO:** Follow the path and enter.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You can talk to the Quest Guide.

### 000.18 — Talk to the Quest Guide
**TARGET:** Quest Guide  
**DO:** Talk to him and continue the required dialogue.  
**NOTE:** Tutorial Island is now tracked as quest **#0, Learning the Ropes**.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** He asks you to view the Quest List/Journal.

### 000.19 — Open the Quest List
**TARGET:** Quest List icon  
**DO:** Click the flashing Quest List icon.  
**REMEMBER:** Red = not started, yellow = in progress, green = completed.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** The tutorial tells you to continue downstairs.

### 000.20 — Climb down the ladder
**TARGET:** Ladder in the Quest Guide building  
**DO:** Climb down into the Tutorial Island dungeon.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You are beside the Mining Instructor.

---

## E — Mining Instructor

### 000.21 — Talk to the Mining Instructor
**TARGET:** Mining Instructor / Dezzick  
**DO:** Talk to Dezzick until he gives you a **bronze pickaxe** and tells you to mine tin and copper.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Bronze pickaxe is in your inventory.

### 000.22 — Mine one tin ore
**TARGET:** Tin rocks to the west  
**DO:** Left-click a tin rock and wait for **1 tin ore**.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Tin ore is in your inventory.

### 000.23 — Mine one copper ore
**TARGET:** Copper rocks to the east  
**DO:** Left-click a copper rock and wait for **1 copper ore**.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You have 1 tin ore + 1 copper ore.

### 000.24 — Smelt one bronze bar
**TARGET:** Furnace to the south  
**REQUIRED:** 1 tin ore + 1 copper ore  
**DO:** Left-click the furnace or use either ore on it. Smelt the ores into **1 bronze bar**.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Bronze bar is in your inventory.

### 000.25 — Talk to Dezzick again
**TARGET:** Mining Instructor  
**DO:** Talk to him after making the bronze bar. Receive a **hammer** and the instruction to smith a dagger.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Hammer is available.

### 000.26 — Smith a bronze dagger
**TARGET:** Nearby anvil  
**REQUIRED:** Hammer + bronze bar  
**DO:** Click the anvil/use the bar on the anvil and select **Bronze dagger**.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Bronze dagger is in your inventory.

### OPTIONAL 000-B — Level 3 Mining and Smithing
**OPTIONAL:** Tutorial Island has ore, furnace and anvil extremely close together. If you specifically want the extra early XP, train **Mining to 3** and **Smithing to 3**, then stop.  
**MAIN ROUTE:** Skip this; it is not required for any upcoming mandatory step.

---

## F — Combat Instructor

### 000.27 — Go through the east gate and talk to Vannaka
**TARGET:** Combat Instructor / Vannaka  
**DO:** Go east through the gate and talk to Vannaka.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** He asks you to equip the bronze dagger.

### 000.28 — Open Worn Equipment and equip the dagger
**TARGET:** Worn Equipment icon, then bronze dagger  
**DO:** Open Worn Equipment as instructed, then click the bronze dagger in your inventory to equip it.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Bronze dagger is equipped.

### 000.29 — Talk to Vannaka again
**TARGET:** Vannaka  
**DO:** Talk to him. Receive a **bronze sword** and **wooden shield**.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Both items are in your inventory.

### 000.30 — Equip sword and shield
**TARGET:** Bronze sword + wooden shield  
**DO:** Click the bronze sword, then the wooden shield to equip both.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Sword and shield are equipped.

### 000.31 — Open Combat Options
**TARGET:** Crossed-swords Combat Options icon  
**DO:** Click the flashing icon and view the available attack styles.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** The tutorial allows you to enter the rat pit.

### 000.32 — Enter the rat pit
**TARGET:** Gate into the giant-rat pen  
**DO:** Open the gate and enter.  
**SAFETY:** The tutorial prevents a normal lethal death in this required melee section.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You are inside the pen.

### 000.33 — Kill one giant rat with melee
**TARGET:** Giant rat (level 3)  
**EQUIPPED:** Bronze sword + wooden shield  
**DO:** Attack one rat and remain in combat until it dies.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Exactly one required melee rat kill is credited.

### 000.34 — Return to Vannaka
**TARGET:** Combat Instructor  
**DO:** Talk to Vannaka after the melee kill. Receive a **shortbow** and **50 bronze arrows**.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Shortbow and arrows are in your inventory.

### 000.35 — Equip shortbow and arrows
**TARGET:** Shortbow + bronze arrows  
**DO:** Equip the bow and then the arrows.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Both are equipped.

### 000.36 — Kill one giant rat with Ranged
**TARGET:** Giant rat inside the pen  
**DO:** From outside the pen, attack a giant rat with the shortbow until it dies.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** The second required giant-rat kill is credited and the tutorial sends you onward.

### OPTIONAL 000-C — Extra Tutorial Island combat XP
**OPTIONAL:** It is possible to manipulate rat HP/worlds to train some combat skills to level 3.  
**MAIN ROUTE:** Do not do this. Our Ironman route will use quest XP to skip weak early melee training.

---

## G — Bank and Account Guide

### 000.37 — Climb the east ladder
**TARGET:** Ladder east of the combat area  
**DO:** Climb up to the surface and enter the nearby bank building.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You can access the bank booth.

### 000.38 — Open the bank
**TARGET:** Bank booth  
**DO:** Click the bank booth. Observe the **25 coins** in your bank. Close the bank when the tutorial has registered the action.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** The bank interface has been opened successfully.

### 000.39 — Use the poll booth
**TARGET:** Poll booth on the western wall  
**DO:** Interact with it when the tutorial asks you to learn about OSRS polls. You do not need to vote.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** The poll tutorial advances.

### 000.40 — Talk to the Account Guide
**TARGET:** Account Guide beyond the eastern door  
**DO:** Enter through the east door and talk to him.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** He asks you to open Account Management.

### 000.41 — Open Account Management
**TARGET:** Person/cog Account Management icon  
**DO:** Click the flashing Account Management icon, then talk to the Account Guide again and continue the required prompts.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** The tutorial directs you to Brother Brace.

---

## H — Brother Brace

### 000.42 — Enter the chapel and talk to Brother Brace
**TARGET:** Brother Brace  
**DO:** Exit east, follow the path to the chapel, enter, and talk to Brother Brace.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** He asks you to open the Prayer interface.

### 000.43 — Open the Prayer interface
**TARGET:** Star-shaped Prayer icon  
**DO:** Click the flashing Prayer icon. Then talk to Brother Brace again and continue the explanation of Prayer, Prayer points, altars and burying bones.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** The south exit becomes the next tutorial route.

---

## I — Magic and quest completion

### 000.44 — Enter the Magic Instructor building
**TARGET:** Building south-east of the chapel  
**DO:** Exit the chapel through the south door, follow the path south-east and enter the final building.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You can talk to the Magic Instructor.

### 000.45 — Talk to the Magic Instructor
**TARGET:** Magic Instructor / Terrova  
**DO:** Talk to him. When instructed, open the Magic interface using the spellbook icon. Talk to him again to receive air and mind runes.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You have the supplied air + mind runes and can select Wind Strike.

### 000.46 — Cast Wind Strike on a chicken
**TARGET:** Chicken in the northern pen  
**REQUIRED:** Wind Strike + supplied air/mind runes  
**DO:** Select **Wind Strike**, then click a chicken in the northern pen.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** The spell is cast and **Learning the Ropes completes**.

## QUEST CHECKPOINT — Learning the Ropes complete

You now have **1 Quest Point**, but **do not leave Tutorial Island yet**. We still need to lock the account into Standard Ironman mode.

---

## J — Standard Ironman setup

### 000.47 — Talk to Terrova after quest completion
**TARGET:** Magic Instructor  
**DO:** Talk to him again. He will prepare you to leave for Lumbridge and give you the chance to speak to the Ironman tutor first. Choose to deal with Ironman mode before leaving.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You are free to go to the Ironman tutor rather than teleporting away.

### 000.48 — Find Paul outside the final building
**TARGET:** Paul, the Ironman tutor  
**DO:** Leave the Magic Instructor building and talk to **Paul**.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** The Ironman setup/options are open.

### 000.49 — Select Standard Ironman
**TARGET:** Ironman mode selection  
**DO:** Choose **Standard Ironman**. Do not choose Hardcore, Ultimate, Group Ironman or a normal account for this route. Confirm the selection requested by the interface.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** The account is registered as **Standard Ironman**.

### 000.50 — Verify the mode before leaving
**TARGET:** Account mode confirmation / Paul  
**DO:** Confirm that Standard Ironman restrictions are active. If you are uncertain, talk to Paul again before leaving.  
**HARD STOP:** Do not teleport to Lumbridge until this is confirmed.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** Standard Ironman is confirmed.

---

## K — Leave Tutorial Island

### 000.51 — Return to the Magic Instructor
**TARGET:** Terrova  
**DO:** Go back inside and talk to him again.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** He instructs you to use **Lumbridge Home Teleport**.

### 000.52 — Cast Lumbridge Home Teleport
**TARGET:** Lumbridge Home Teleport in the Magic interface  
**DO:** Open the Magic interface and select **Lumbridge Home Teleport**. Let the teleport animation finish; do not cancel it by moving or clicking elsewhere.  
**COMPLETE WHEN:** You arrive in Lumbridge.

---

# CHECKPOINT 000 — Tutorial Island finished

**Location:** Lumbridge  
**Account mode:** Standard Ironman  
**Quest:** Learning the Ropes — complete  
**Quest Points:** 1

## Exact expected starter inventory

The game standardises your post-Tutorial-Island starter state. You should own:

- 1 bronze axe
- 1 bronze pickaxe
- 1 tinderbox
- 1 small fishing net
- 1 shrimps
- 1 bronze dagger
- 1 bronze sword
- 1 wooden shield
- 1 shortbow
- 25 bronze arrows
- 25 air runes
- 15 mind runes
- 1 bucket
- 1 pot
- 1 bread
- 6 water runes
- 4 earth runes
- 2 body runes

**Bank:** 25 coins

Extra Tutorial Island items you tried to hoard are not part of the mainland state, so the route will never depend on them.

## STOP HERE

Do not start wandering around Lumbridge yet. **Step 001** will begin from this exact checkpoint and will explain the Lumbridge route from zero.
